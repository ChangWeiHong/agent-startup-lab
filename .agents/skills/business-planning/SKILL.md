# business-planning

**Use when:** Prepare an experiment-sized lean business plan

## Inputs
- Opportunity and validation evidence
- Existing alternatives, reachable buyer and possible business model
- Unit economics and legal/platform risks

## Steps
1. Fill one-page plan with known vs unknown clearly separated
2. Compare subscription vs alternative billing by value frequency
3. Define one measurable product outcome and distribution assumption
4. Specify smallest next investment and decision gate

## Outputs / acceptance
- Lean plan linked to evidence and related Issue
- Top risk and next action, not a speculative multi-year forecast

## Gates
Read [work contract](../../../contracts/work-item.md) and [evidence](../../../contracts/evidence.md). Never claim fabricated verification, spend funds, contact customers, deploy or manipulate private data without explicit authorization. Return GitHub links when supported; otherwise structured handoff.
