# market-validation

**Use when:** Design and evaluate a real willingness-to-pay experiment

## Inputs
- Opportunity and payment hypothesis
- Evidence/experiment template
- Approved time and cash constraints

## Steps
1. Choose outreach, concierge, landing page, or minimal live test
2. Predeclare expected observable paid/intention signals and sample limits
3. Gather only authorized data and distinguish evidence strength
4. Produce next decision with falsification notes

## Outputs / acceptance
- Experiment record + measured funnel with denominators
- Explicit confidence/limitations and scale/iterate/kill proposal

## Gates
Read [work contract](../../../contracts/work-item.md) and [evidence](../../../contracts/evidence.md). Never claim fabricated verification, spend funds, contact customers, deploy or manipulate private data without explicit authorization. Return GitHub links when supported; otherwise structured handoff.
