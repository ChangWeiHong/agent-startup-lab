# 01 Discover — find testable opportunities

**Input:** target geography/audience, channels you can access, time and spend constraints.

1. Collect pain from support forums, app reviews, communities, search demand, people you can interview, manual business workflows and credible revenue case studies.
2. For each candidate, record **buyer**, exact moment of pain, current workaround, frequency, desired outcome and possible existing payment.
3. Identify competitors including free and manual substitutes; note observation date and source.
4. Map likely distribution: who can you reach without already being famous or funding broad ads?
5. Create an Opportunity issue and an optional [opportunity record](../templates/opportunity.md). Avoid writing a full PRD.
6. Advance only candidates with a small falsifiable experiment.

**Exit:** short list with cited evidence and a reachable channel. Unsourced earnings anecdotes stay labeled claims.
