# 02 Evaluate — prioritize hypotheses

1. Use [scorecard](../strategy/venture-scorecard.md) with evidence links.
2. State a falsifiable buyer/payment hypothesis: “We believe [segment] will pay [price] to [outcome] when [trigger].”
3. Compare realistic alternatives, frequency of value and local/global pricing assumptions.
4. Estimate distribution effort, implementation burden, variable unit costs, data/security and legal risks.
5. Pick a first test designed to **disprove** the most dangerous assumption for the least time and cash.
6. Decide: reject, hold, or open a Validation issue.

**Exit:** proposed experiment with success and failure indicators. A score is not evidence of actual demand.
