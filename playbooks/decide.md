# 08 Decide — scale, iterate, pause or kill

At the precommitted evaluation date, review:
1. Customer pain and reachability confirmed?
2. Payment/purchase behavior observed? What exactly was charged or promised?
3. Repeat usage / retention measured where time allows?
4. Can contribution-margin economics plausibly cover CAC and support cost?
5. Security, compliance and support risks manageable?
6. What did we learn; can it be reused for other ventures?

**Scale:** multiple reliable signals and manageable unit economics; increase investment incrementally with explicit budget approval.
**Iterate:** credible problem, specific diagnosable weakness and inexpensive next test.
**Pause:** evidence incomplete; define exactly what would restart it.
**Kill:** failed critical assumption, unreachable buyer, weak payment signals after adequate exposure or economics structurally poor.

Write a [decision record](../templates/decision-record.md) including rejected alternatives and evidence. Mark GitHub Issue status accordingly; archive the lesson instead of erasing it.
