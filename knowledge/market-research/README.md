# Market research — SOP

**Research question:** who experiences what recurring pain, when, with which current workaround, and who can pay to change that?

1. Search real user complaints, app reviews, community discussions, professional workflows and competitor pages. Record accessible geography and date.
2. Find existing alternatives, including free tools, human service, spreadsheets, WhatsApp and doing nothing.
3. Identify *trigger*, pain severity, frequency, purchasing decision-maker, budget and measurable result.
4. Test distribution access. Being able to build a tool does not mean you can acquire users.
5. Check recurring value, legal/platform constraints, data/privacy handling and variable AI/API costs.
6. Make an Opportunity issue. State what would falsify the hypothesis.

## Evidence strengths

Observed payment / paid pilot > sourced transaction or sales data > specific prospect price acceptance > repeated actual usage > qualified signups > clicks. All research sources must carry dates and limitations.

Competitor marketing claims, founder self-reported MRR and industry statistics are **unverified third-party claims** unless a direct credible source supports them; a competitor's success never establishes your conversion rate.
