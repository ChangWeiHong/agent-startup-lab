# Pricing and business economics

Subscriptions are appropriate for **repeated value**, not automatically for a one-time file conversion. Test monthly/yearly/usage/one-off options aligned with the job.

## Questions

- Who pays (user or organization)? What is the current cost of the alternative?
- What tangible time/money/risk does the result save? How often?
- Is there an ongoing reason to return? What is the cancellation trigger?
- Can a Malaysian localized offer be priced by observed willingness to pay rather than direct USD conversion?
- What costs scale with each paying user (LLM inference, storage, support, payment fees)?

## Decision metrics

- `Gross revenue = actual collected sales`; distinguish one-time sales from MRR.
- `Net contribution = collected revenue − fees − variable hosting/AI/support costs`.
- `Gross churn = lost paying subscribers / payers at period start` (record definitions).
- `CAC = attributable acquisition cost / new paying customers`.
- `Contribution-margin LTV` requires observed cohort survival and contribution assumptions; new-app LTV is uncertain.

## Pricing experiment

Quote an actual price and ask for a small real commitment if permitted. Track price, audience, responses and **verified actual purchases**. “Looks affordable” is weaker evidence than payment.

**Testing range ≠ accepted target:** RM15–30/month B2C and RM30–100/month for tiny professional utilities may be *initial hypotheses* only. Do not claim they are optimal or already validated by this venture.
