# Opportunity scorecard

Use scores as **prioritization**, not proof. For each criterion, record evidence and uncertainty.

| Criterion | Question | 0–3 scoring anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pain / urgency | Does someone actively struggle today? | 0 conjecture; 1 anecdote; 2 repeated reports; 3 observed costly workaround |
| Reachability | Can we speak to potential buyers this week? | 0 unknown; 1 broad channel; 2 specific community; 3 direct contacts / verified intent |
| Paid intent | Are buyers paying for substitutes or committing? | 0 none; 1 analogous pricing; 2 direct price acceptance; 3 paid pilot/preorder |
| Repeated value | Why would users pay again next month? | 0 one-off; 1 uncertain; 2 recurring use case; 3 recurring usage observed |
| Distinctiveness | Why not use a free or incumbent option? | 0 unclear; 1 features; 2 workflow fit; 3 tested differentiated outcome |
| Cost/complexity | Can a safe MVP be tested cheaply? | 0 infeasible; 1 heavy; 2 modest; 3 no/low hosting |

Score with a **dated evidence link** and identify fatal assumptions separately (legal risk, proprietary access, no reachable buyer, unacceptable API costs). Do not let a high total override a fatal risk.

Rank candidates for **next validation action**, not for shipping. Review after each new piece of evidence.
