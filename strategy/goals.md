# Venture strategy (editable)

**Mission:** identify a sustainable paid product or service with minimal cash burn while preserving reliability and security for real users.

## Initial focus

- Start with a pool of 3–5 cheaply testable **hypotheses**, not simultaneous production products.
- Prefer narrowly defined problems, reachable users and repeatable value.
- Malaysia-first is an option, not a requirement; test pricing and distribution locally instead of assuming localization alone creates a moat.
- Default monetization preference: subscriptions where value recurs. Consider usage, service or one-time payments if retention cannot justify a subscription.
- No ads-as-revenue business model at the outset; ads may be used for acquisition.
- Prioritize **first verified payment / repeat intent**, then sustainable margin, retention and eventually meaningful MRR.

## Editable success metrics

Primary: qualified conversations, price acceptance, genuine paid conversions, repeat usage, retention and contribution margin.
Secondary: impressions, CTR, CPC, email signups and downloads. **Never promote these to proof of willingness to pay.**

## Portfolio rule

Explore several opportunities; deeply build at most one or two at a time. Once a winner has repeatable acquisition and retention signals, redirect most effort there. The precise percentage is a planning choice, not a universal benchmark.
